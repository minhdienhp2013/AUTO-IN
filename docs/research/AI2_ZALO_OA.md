## CODE-402 follow-up — synthetic signed structured postback (13/10/2026)

- `SyntheticFixtureNormalizer` (called **after** synthetic verifier) now accepts a separate fixture `kind:"postback"` with **exactly** `confirmation_postback:{kind:"verified_confirmation_accept",challenge_id:<canonical UUID>,challenge_token:<43-char canonical base64url of 32 random bytes>}`. No text/file/filename/MIME on a postback, no extra or missing challenge fields. Wrong structure, oversized/short/noncanonical token and text/file fields **on a `kind:"postback"` fixture** are **422 INVALID_WEBHOOK**.
